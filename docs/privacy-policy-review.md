# Privacy policy review: draft vs. actual implementation

**Date:** 2026-11-22 · **Reviewed:** `docs/privacy-policy.md` (draft v1, 2026-11-22), compared with the code in `spikes/article25-core-spike` (dev v3.5.0), `spikes/article25-ai-spike/forge-llm-harness`, and live behaviour on the demo site.
**Status:** internal engineering review. **This is not legal advice.** Nothing here has been published, and the policy URL is not live.

Legend: ❌ inaccurate or missing in draft v1 · ⚠️ incomplete or needs care · ✅ accurate · ⚖️ needs a lawyer or privacy professional

## 1. Findings: draft v1 statements vs. implementation

| # | Draft v1 says | What the code actually does | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "No data is sent to servers operated by the developer or to third parties." | No egress: there is no `external.fetch` in any manifest. **However**, the spike logs every resolver payload and output (`src/index.js` evidence wrapper, up to 3,500 chars) plus validator and trigger events. Payloads include account IDs, issue keys, review conditions, screening evidence quotes and, in tests, page text. Atlassian's docs say Forge logs are viewable by the developer in the developer console, including production, and that log access is **"enabled by default when a user installs your app"** (site admins can turn it off). The developer can therefore see this data through Atlassian. | ❌ The product build **must** remove payload/output logging (log IDs, outcomes, counts, timings only). The policy must disclose operational logs. |
| 2 | Decision data: "work-item key, decision state, conditions, reviewer Atlassian account ID, timestamps, and a cryptographic signature." | Each decision is also a **hash-chain record** `{siteId (cloudId), seq, prevHash, decisionId, issueKey, state, conditions, decidedBy, decidedAt, sig, hash}`. ## 2. Security limits that should be disclosed (plain language, no guarantees)
- Workflow gating depends on admins adding the Article25 validator to workflows; un-gated transitions are not controlled.
- The gate **fails closed**: if the app errors, is misconfigured or is uninstalled, gated transitions are blocked.
- The decision backup on Confluence is tamper-**evident**, not tamper-proof. Edits, deletions, reordering and replays are detected and refused at recovery. A user with space-admin rights who consistently rolls back the backup, its page history and work-item receipts to an earlier state can make an older history look complete. Recovery therefore requires a named recovery-admin group and a two-step confirmation, and every attempt is audited.
- Screening is assistive and can miss personal-data processing or flag it wrongly. It does not replace a privacy professional's judgement.

## 3. Items requiring legal / privacy-professional review (⚖️)
1. Developer role (controller / processor / neither) for a no-egress Forge app, and whether a DPA is needed or offered.
2. Whether developer access to Forge logs (default-on) creates processing by the developer, and how to describe it (strongly mitigated by removing content logging; see #1).
3. Wording of rights and requests sections; jurisdiction and governing law; age/children statement (probably N/A for B2B).
4. Forge LLM: confirm Atlassian's published terms (training, retention, region, sub-processors) before any AI statement beyond "processed by Atlassian".
5. Retention of reviewer account IDs in the customer-owned Confluence backup after uninstall (customer-controlled, but must be clearly disclosed).
6. Whether the product name "Article25" or marketing copy could be read as a GDPR compliance claim; add a disclaimer.
7. Any statement about data residency, "Runs on Atlassian", security certifications or Marketplace programs. **We hold no certifications.**

## 4. Engineering actions this review creates (for the product build; not started)
- Remove payload/output logging and keep structured, content-free logs (P0, before any judge install).
- Scope screening to configured projects; add an "AI off" default.
- Remove spike-only features (tamper button, simulate hook, expression validator, v1 mirror) from the judge build.
- Index receipts via a `jira:entityProperty` so recovery can enumerate *all* receipts, not only those of issues named in the backup.
- Data deletion helper: "remove Article25 data from this site" (issue properties + register-page property) for admins.

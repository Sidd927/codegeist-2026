## Limitation (be aware before publishing)
The sound effects' licence is documented by the toolchain as a set; the individual Pixabay source pages are not recorded.
- If you want per-file provenance, you have two choices:
  - replace each effect with one you download yourself from Pixabay and note its URL;
  - render a narration-only mix by deleting the effect `<audio>` tags in `film/index.html` (or their lines in `film/build.py`) and re-rendering.
- Either needs about 15 minutes of render time.
